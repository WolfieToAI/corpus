# Remote work and benefits policy - Northwind Germany

This policy applies to employees on a German employment contract (Munich office). Employees on
a French contract follow the French policy.

## Remote work

Employees in Germany may work remotely up to 2 days per week. Monday and Thursday are on-site
days in Munich. Working remotely from abroad is limited to 10 working days per calendar year
and requires written approval from HR.

## Home office equipment

Employees in Germany receive a one-off home office allowance of 400 euros. There is no monthly
remote work allowance; instead, employees can claim the home office tax deduction in their
annual tax return.

## Leave

- Paid holidays: 30 working days per year.
- Parental leave: employees take Elternzeit under German law; Northwind Germany adds a bonus of
  2,000 euros at the birth or adoption of a child.
- Sick leave: a medical certificate is required from the third day of absence.

## Expenses

Expenses must be filed within 60 days with a receipt. Flights are allowed only for trips over
six hours by train.

## Notice period

The notice period follows the German Civil Code and is at least four weeks to the 15th or the
end of a calendar month, increasing with seniority.
