# Northwind R5 robot vacuum - user manual

## Overview

The Northwind R5 is our entry-level robot vacuum for apartments up to 90 square metres. It uses
gyroscope navigation and a single bristle brush, and charges on a compact dock without
self-emptying. This manual covers the R5 only; the flagship R7 has its own manual.

## Battery and charging

The R5 uses a 2600 mAh lithium-ion battery. A full charge of the R5 takes about 6 hours. On a
full battery the R5 cleans for up to 90 minutes in normal mode. When the battery is low the
robot goes back to its dock, but unlike the R7 it does not resume the cleaning run afterwards:
start a new run from the app once it is charged.

## Connectivity

The R5 connects to the Northwind app over Wi-Fi and only supports 2.4 GHz networks. If your
router broadcasts a single network name for both bands, create a dedicated 2.4 GHz network
before pairing the R5. The R5 stores a single floor map.

## Maintenance schedule

- Empty the dust bin of the R5 after every cleaning run.
- Replace the R5 filter every 2 months.
- Replace the R5 bristle brush every 6 months.
- Clean the cliff sensors once a month with a dry cloth.
- Check the front wheel for hair every two weeks.

The R5 filter can be rinsed with cold water once a month; let it dry for 24 hours before
refitting it.

## Error codes

- E-101: brush jammed. Remove and clean the bristle brush.
- E-204: cliff sensor dirty. Wipe the three sensors under the robot.
- E-330: bumper stuck. Tap the front bumper gently until it moves freely.
- E-460: low battery cannot start. Place the R5 on its dock and wait for 30 minutes.

## Warranty

The R5 is covered by a 2-year warranty from the date of purchase, battery included.
Consumables such as filters and brushes are not covered.
