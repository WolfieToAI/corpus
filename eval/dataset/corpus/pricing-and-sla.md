# Northwind Fleet - plans, pricing and service level agreement

## Plans

- Starter: 49 euros per month, up to 5 robots, email support, 30 days of mission history.
- Business: 199 euros per month, up to 50 robots, Fleet API access, phone support, 1 year of
  mission history.
- Enterprise: custom pricing for more than 50 robots, single sign-on, a dedicated customer
  success manager and unlimited history.

Annual billing gives two months free on Starter and Business. Prices exclude VAT.

## Service level agreement

The monthly availability commitment of the Fleet platform is 99.9 percent on the Business plan
and 99.95 percent on the Enterprise plan. The Starter plan has no availability commitment.

If availability falls below the commitment, customers receive service credits: 10 percent of
the monthly fee below the commitment, and 25 percent of the monthly fee if availability falls
below 99 percent. Credits must be requested within 30 days after the end of the month and
cannot be exchanged for cash.

Scheduled maintenance announced at least 72 hours in advance is excluded from availability
calculations.

## Support response times

- Enterprise: response within 1 hour for critical incidents, 24 hours a day.
- Business: response within 4 business hours.
- Starter: response within 2 business days.
