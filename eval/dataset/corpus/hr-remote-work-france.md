# Remote work and benefits policy - Northwind France

This policy applies to employees on a French employment contract (Lyon and Paris offices). The
policy for employees on a German contract is a separate document.

## Remote work

Employees in France may work remotely up to 3 days per week, agreed with their manager. Tuesday
is an on-site day for every team, so that each team meets in person at least once a week.
Working remotely from another country is allowed for up to 20 working days per calendar year
and must be declared to HR at least two weeks in advance, for tax and social security reasons.

## Home office equipment

Every employee in France receives a one-off home office allowance of 600 euros to buy a chair,
a desk or a monitor. A monthly remote work allowance of 30 euros is added to the payslip to
cover electricity and internet costs. Laptops and headsets are provided by IT and remain
company property.

## Leave

- Paid holidays: 25 working days per year, plus 10 RTT days for employees on a fixed-days
  contract.
- Parental leave: Northwind France tops up the statutory scheme so that the second parent
  receives full pay for 16 weeks.
- Sick leave: full salary is maintained from the first day of absence, with no waiting period.

## Expenses

Business expenses must be submitted in the expense tool within 30 days with a receipt.
Expenses submitted after 90 days are not reimbursed. Train is mandatory for trips under four
hours; first class is allowed for journeys over three hours.

## Notice period

The notice period for employees in France is one month during the first year and three months
afterwards, for both resignation and dismissal, except in case of gross misconduct.
