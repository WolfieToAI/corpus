# Northwind R7 robot vacuum - user manual

## Overview

The Northwind R7 is our flagship robot vacuum for homes up to 250 square metres. It combines
LiDAR navigation, a dual rubber brush and a self-emptying dock. This manual covers setup,
daily use, maintenance and troubleshooting for the R7 only; the entry-level R5 has its own
manual.

## Battery and charging

The R7 uses a 5200 mAh lithium-ion battery. A full charge of the R7 takes about 4 hours on the
standard dock. On a full battery the R7 cleans for up to 180 minutes in Eco mode and about
110 minutes in Turbo mode. When the battery drops below 15 percent the robot returns to its
dock on its own, recharges to 80 percent and then resumes the cleaning run where it stopped.

Do not leave the R7 off its dock for more than three months: a deeply discharged battery can
no longer be recovered and is not covered by the warranty.

## Connectivity

The R7 connects to the Northwind app over Wi-Fi. It supports both 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz networks
and WPA3 encryption. Maps are stored on the robot and synchronised to the app; up to five
floor maps can be saved, which suits multi-storey homes.

## Maintenance schedule

Regular maintenance keeps suction strong and extends the life of the robot.

- Empty the dock bag when the app warns you, typically every 6 to 8 weeks.
- Replace the R7 HEPA filter every 3 months.
- Replace the main rubber brush of the R7 every 12 months.
- Wipe the LiDAR cover and the cliff sensors with a dry microfibre cloth once a month.
- Replace the side brush every 6 months, or sooner if its bristles are bent.

Never wash the HEPA filter with water: it destroys the filtering membrane.

## Error codes

The R7 reports problems with a code in the app and a voice prompt.

- E-101: main brush jammed. Remove the brush, cut away tangled hair and refit it.
- E-204: cliff sensor dirty. Clean the four sensors under the robot with a dry cloth.
- E-317: drive wheel stuck. Lift the robot and check that nothing blocks the wheels.
- E-450: dock not found. Make sure the dock is powered and has 50 cm of free space on each
  side.
- E-512: LiDAR blocked. Remove any object pressing on the LiDAR turret on top of the robot.

If an error persists after the suggested fix, restart the robot by holding the power button
for 10 seconds, then contact support with the error code.

## Warranty

The R7 is covered by a 3-year warranty on the robot body and a 1-year warranty on the battery
from the date of purchase. Consumables such as filters, brushes and dock bags are not covered.
Proof of purchase is required for every warranty claim.
